# Phase 2b - Final Presentation Content

This file gives the content of every slide of the final presentation, in the order of the Addendum A layout. Build it in presentation software with one slide for each section below.

**Rules applied to every slide**

- **7x7 rule:** no more than seven bullet points per slide and no more than seven words per bullet point.
- **Bullet format:** all points on the content slides are bullet points, with the same bullet style throughout.
- **Acknowledgement:** every graph, image and source used on a slide is acknowledged on that slide (a small caption) and in the references on the last slide.
- **Own words:** the findings are written in the learner's own words, not copied from a source.
- **Fill-ins:** the blanks (____) must be completed by the learner, because they depend on personal details or on the questionnaire results. Replace each blank with the real information.
- **Navigation, effects and spelling:** hyperlinks, action buttons, transitions, animation and the spelling check are added in the next goal ("Presentation navigation, effects and polish").
