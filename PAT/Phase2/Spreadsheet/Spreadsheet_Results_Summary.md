# Phase 2a - Spreadsheet Results Summary

This summary turns the questionnaire results into points for the findings slides of the final presentation.

**How to use it:** The questionnaire data is captured on the **Data** worksheet and counted on the **Analysis** worksheet. Once all ten completed questionnaires have been captured, copy each figure from the cell named below into the blanks (____), and delete the options in brackets that do not apply. Then rewrite each finding in your own words for the slides, because the findings slides must be written in the learner's own words.

**Sample:** ____ learners (Analysis!B86), made up of ____ males, ____ females and ____ who preferred not to say (Analysis!B6:B8), from grades ____ (Analysis!B13:B17).

## Overall conclusion for the presentation

Write two or three sentences in your own words that answer the focus question using the findings above:

> *How is modern technology helping to reduce the effects of climate change, and which solutions are the most effective?*

Your conclusion should:

1. Say what most learners think about whether technology can help.
2. Name the technology that learners chose as most effective, and say whether it matches your Phase 1 research.
3. Mention one point from the household or personal-use findings (Findings 4 and 5).

## Notes for the slides

- Use at most one or two findings slides, in line with the presentation requirements, and choose the findings that best support your conclusion.
- Keep to the 7x7 rule: no more than seven bullet points per slide and no more than seven words per bullet point.
- Insert the graphs as pictures saved from the Graphs worksheet, and make sure that each one has a title and labelled axes.
- Acknowledge the questionnaire as a primary source on the references slide.
